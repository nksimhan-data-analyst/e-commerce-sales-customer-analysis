# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analysis

## 1. Project Objective

Analyze e-commerce sales, customers, products, geography, discounts, profitability, and shipping data to identify meaningful business trends and opportunities.

## 2. Business Questions

### Sales Performance

* How does revenue change over time?
* What are the monthly and quarterly sales trends?
* What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
* Which categories generate the most revenue and profit?

### Product Performance

* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which products generate the most profit?
* Which products have high revenue but low profit margins?

### Customer Analysis

* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
* Which customer segments are declining?
* What proportion of customers are repeat customers?

### Geographic Analysis

* Which regions, states, or cities generate the most revenue?
* Which geographic areas generate the most profit?
* Are there locations with high revenue but relatively low profitability?

### Discount Analysis

* How do discounts affect revenue?
* How do discounts affect profit margins?
* Which categories are most affected by discounting?

### Shipping Analysis

* Which shipping methods are most frequently used?
* Which shipping methods have the highest costs?
* Does shipping performance vary by geography or order value?
